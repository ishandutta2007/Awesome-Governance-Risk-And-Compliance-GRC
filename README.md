# Awesome-Governance-Risk-And-Compliance-GRC

## Top Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Integrated Risk Management, Regulatory Compliance & Audit Automation*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)**. These tools enable organizations to unify risk registers, control frameworks, policy management, vendor assessments, and audit workflows—moving from spreadsheet-driven compliance to continuous, evidence-based governance.



**Examples** include ServiceNow Governance Risk and Compliance, MetricStream, Archer, Workiva, AuditBoard, LogicGate, Riskonnect, Diligent HighBond, SAI360, and OneTrust (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosted GRC platforms, framework mapping, and AI-ready compliance automation—ideal for organizations building sovereign risk and compliance infrastructure without per-module or per-seat licensing lock-in.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[ServiceNow Governance Risk and Compliance](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  

  Enterprise GRC on the Now Platform with Integrated Risk Management covering risk registers, policy and compliance management, audit management, and vendor risk; integrates with CMDB for business-context risk scoring and regulatory change tracking.



- **[MetricStream](https://www.metricstream.com/)**  

  AI-powered GRC platform recognized as a Category Leader across all seven GRC categories by Chartis Research; features continuous risk sensing, AI assistants for risk and compliance workflows, autonomous evidence gathering, and outcome assurance for remediation validation.



- **[Archer](https://www.archerirm.com/)**  

  Enterprise GRC platform with integrated risk register, quantitative risk insights with dollar-value exposure, third-party risk governance, and audit-ready evidence with timestamped trails; serves 1,500+ customers across 48 countries including 50% of Fortune 500.



- **[Workiva](https://www.workiva.com/)**  

  Financial compliance and SEC reporting platform with ICFR and SOX compliance capabilities; provides a single source of truth with data traceability and streamlined audit interactions.



- **[AuditBoard](https://www.auditboard.com/)**  

  Audit management platform supporting the full audit lifecycle from planning and risk assessment through workpaper management, issue tracking, and reporting.



- **[LogicGate](https://www.logicgate.ai/)**  

  AI GRC platform with GRC Agents for ERM, TPRM, AI Governance, and Business Continuity Management; features Automated Control Testing with detailed rationale, Bulk Record Linking, and upcoming MCP Server Access for natural language Risk Cloud queries.



- **[Riskonnect](https://www.riskonnect.com/)**  

  Integrated risk management platform covering ERM, operational risk, compliance, audit, and business continuity with a unified risk register and reporting dashboards.



- **[Diligent HighBond](https://www.diligent.com/)**  

  GRC platform combining audit, risk, and compliance capabilities with analytics and reporting for boards and executive teams.



- **[SAI360](https://www.sai360.com/)**  

  GRC and compliance platform with operational risk management, control testing, regulatory change management, and ethics/compliance training integration.



- **[OneTrust](https://www.onetrust.com/)**  

  GRC platform named a Leader in the IDC MarketScape for Worldwide GRC Software 2025; unifies privacy, AI governance, risk management, and third-party monitoring with automated processes, AI-powered data governance, and no-code workflow automation; trusted by 14,000+ global brands.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[CISO Assistant](https://github.com/intuitem/ciso-assistant-community)**  

  The most comprehensive open-source GRC platform (AGPL-3.0); one-stop-shop for Risk Management, AppSec, Compliance & Audit, TPRM, Privacy, and Reporting; supports **220+ global frameworks and 45+ mapping sets with automatic control mapping**, including ISO 27001, NIST CSF, SOC 2, PCI DSS, NIS2, DORA, GDPR, HIPAA, and CMMC; covers EBIOS RM, business impact analysis, cyber risk quantification, incident and vulnerability management, workflow automation, REST API, MCP support, and an AI assistant; rated ~95% coverage of typical GRC requirements in independent comparisons .



- **[Probo](https://github.com/getprobo/probo)**  

  Self-hostable GRC platform (ISC licensed) built for engineering and security teams; covers the full lifecycle: risk register with inherent/residual scoring and treatment strategies, control library with maturity levels, vendor risk, data privacy (DPIA/TIA), access reviews, audit programs, and document approval workflows; exposes **270+ MCP tools** for AI agent automation, `prb` CLI with 44+ command groups, GraphQL API, and n8n community node; Docker-deployable with SSO/SCIM support .



- **[Cairn](https://github.com/frousselet/cairn)**  

  Self-hosted GRC platform (GNU AGPL) with governance (ISO 27001 clause 4 context), assets with CIA valuation, risk management using **ISO 27005 and EBIOS RM**, compliance frameworks with inter-framework mappings, incidents with sealed evidence and statutory notification clocks (GDPR, NIS2, DORA), and a public Trust Center; every feature is API-accessible via REST and built-in MCP server; optional AI (off by default) supports Mistral, OpenAI, Claude, or local Ollama; single-container Docker deployment .



- **[Eramba](https://github.com/eramba/eramba)**  

  Mature open-source GRC software with a free Community edition (no user or data limits) and affordable Enterprise edition; provides risk management, compliance management, policy management, internal controls, incident management, online questionnaires, awareness programs, and REST APIs; supports ISO 27001, PCI DSS, and GDPR frameworks; trusted by hundreds of organizations .



- **[SimpleRisk](https://github.com/simplerisk/simplerisk)**  

  Free, open-source risk management platform for identifying, assessing, prioritizing, and tracking security and operational risks; provides risk mitigation tracking, security controls and compliance management, and centralized visibility into organizational risks; Docker-deployable; rated ~80% coverage of GRC requirements in independent comparisons .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Unicis** — Apache 2.0 licensed open-source compliance platform for startups and SMEs; covers ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, NIS2, and more with self-hosting, EU cloud, and air-gapped deployment options; positions itself as a transparent alternative to Vanta/Drata with predictable flat pricing .

- **OpenGRC** — Simple-interface GRC tool for small businesses and teams new to GRC; supports quick framework imports; rated ~55% coverage in independent comparisons .

- **GovReady-Q** — DevSecOps-oriented GRC platform for fast authorization processes and automated assessments; rated ~60% coverage .

- **ComplianceAsCode** — Security compliance content for various platforms (2.4k GitHub stars); strong for generating compliance artifacts and automation content, not a full GRC suite .



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **CISO Assistant** for comprehensive framework mapping and risk management across 220+ standards, **Probo** for AI-native GRC automation with MCP tooling and audit-ready evidence chains, **Cairn** for ISO 27005/EBIOS RM-aligned risk assessment with regulatory compliance mapping, and **Eramba** for a mature, community-tested foundation in a fully self-hosted GRC stack.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- GRC platforms handling risk assessments, audit evidence, and regulatory data must comply with applicable regulations (SOX, GDPR, DORA, NIS2, HIPAA, etc.) and organizational governance policies; evidence integrity and audit trails are critical for regulatory defensibility.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions vary significantly in scope—CISO Assistant provides the broadest framework coverage and feature depth, while Probo and Cairn offer modern AI-native architectures; Eramba and SimpleRisk provide mature, stable foundations. Full enterprise GRC deployments may still require customization for industry-specific regulatory calculations and auditor collaboration workflows.

- Verify license terms for commercial use; CISO Assistant uses AGPL-3.0 , Probo uses ISC , Cairn uses GNU AGPL , and Eramba offers a free Community edition with paid Enterprise edition .
